About the initiative

Expanding equitable access to STEM across Howard County.

STEM Forward is a community-based CAPA-HC project focused on underrepresented and underserved students. It builds on TechGirls and is supported by $1,046,000 in FY2026 federal Community Project Funding administered by NASA.

Our mission

Bring hands-on STEM learning opportunities to students across Howard County. Through coding, robotics, engineering, earth science, and project-based activities, students build curiosity, confidence, creativity, and essential problem-solving skills.

CAPA-HC — the Chinese American Parent Association of Howard County — is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit founded in 2015. Its three core services are Family & Youth Education, Community Outreach, and Civic Engagement. STEM Forward sits inside that education mission and is open to the broader Howard County community.

What we believe

  • Every child deserves early, inspiring, and equitable STEM opportunities.
  • Near-peer tutors can spark confidence in ways a worksheet cannot.
  • High school mentors grow as leaders while younger students grow as scientists.
  • School partnerships make enrichment sustainable.

Programs

Free after-school STEM, plus camps, field trips, and research support.

The flagship program is a 10-week after-school workshop taught by trained high school tutors. Broader offerings are coming online as STEM Forward scales.

Flagship

After-school STEM workshops

Hands-on classes in partnership with Howard County elementary schools. Sessions are free, 1.5 hours each week, for 10 weeks in Fall and 10 weeks in Spring.

  • Construct a prosthetic hand
  • Build a popsicle-stick bridge
  • Tinker with Snap Circuits
  • Run chemistry and earth-science labs
  • Practice teamwork through games and design challenges

Elementary student registration will open soon.

Typical semester

10 weekly lessons • 1.5 hours after school • taught by a team of high school tutors • one field trip per semester • end-of-year graduation ceremony

The future of innovation starts here.

STEM Forward is a CAPA-HC initiative that brings free after-school STEM learning to 4th- and 5th-grade students in underserved Howard County elementary schools. High school tutors design the lessons. Curiosity does the rest.

What we do

Hands-on STEM that sparks curiosity and builds confidence.

Through coding, robotics, engineering, earth science, and other project-based activities, students build creativity, collaboration, and essential problem-solving skills. The program grows out of TechGirls, CAPA-HC’s after-school STEM workshops for elementary students, and expands that model across more schools and grade bands.

Elementary student registration will open soon. Tutor applications are open now.

Who it serves

A pipeline from elementary school to college.

Elementary students

Weekly after-school labs that strengthen critical thinking, problem-solving, creativity, and interest in future STEM learning.

Middle schoolers

Mentorship and support for science fair research, prototyping, workspace, and project development.

High school tutors

Paid or service-hour roles teaching weekly lessons, mentoring younger students, and building leadership skills.

College mentors

Curriculum support, tutor training, and hands-on coaching that connects every stage of the pipeline.

Parent, TechGirls graduation

Built on TechGirls

A proven after-school model, expanded.

TechGirls launched in the 2021–22 school year. STEM Forward keeps that tutor-led workshop model and, in Fall 2026, runs ten after-school sites: Bryant Woods, Running Brook, Centennial Lane, St. John’s Lane, Thunder Hill, Talbott Springs, Northfield, Veterans, Longfellow, and Bellows Spring.

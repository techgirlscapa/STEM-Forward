High school tutors

Teach labs. Mentor kids. Grow as a leader.

STEM Forward, a CAPA-HC initiative funded by a NASA-administered federal grant, expands access to STEM education through mentorship and hands-on enrichment. All high schoolers are welcome to apply.

Apply now

The role

Our free after-school program empowers 4th- and 5th-grade kids in underserved Howard County elementary schools by providing a fun, supportive environment that sparks curiosity and encourages exploration in STEM.

Whether you love STEM or have a passion for teaching, you will work alongside peers to:

  • Design engaging, hands-on lesson plans
  • Guide kids through labs and activities
  • Develop leadership and presentation skills

What to expect

Training. All tutors attend a mandatory 2-hour training covering teaching, classroom management, and STEM communication.

Commitment. Two sessions per school year (Fall and Spring), with 10 weekly 1.5-hour lessons per semester. Commit to at least one session; a full year is encouraged. Tutors are also asked to support one field trip per semester and the annual graduation ceremony.

Location. Tutors are assigned to a school, typically with two other tutors. You arrange transportation; carpooling is often available.

Questions: stemforward@capa-hc.org

Why tutor with STEM Forward

Service hours or pay

Earn community service hours or compensation while doing work that looks strong on college applications.

Real teaching practice

Training covers STEM instruction, leadership, communication, classroom support, and mentoring younger students.

A team at each school

You are not alone in the room. Most sites run with a small tutor team and college mentor support.
